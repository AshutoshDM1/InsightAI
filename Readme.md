## ✨ Overview

Insight AI is a modern AI platform that combines stunning visuals with seamless performance. Our mission is to provide AI enthusiasts with an immersive reading experience through an intuitive interface and comprehensive library.

## 🚀 Features

- **AI-Powered Chat**: Engage in intelligent conversations with our advanced AI assistant
- **Text-to-Text Response**: Get instant, contextual responses to your queries
- **Responsive Design**: Seamless chat experience across all devices
- **User Authentication**: Secure Google sign-in with personalized profiles
- **Real-time Interaction**: Instant AI responses with smooth typing animations
- **Interactive UI**: Modern interface with animated gradients and smooth transitions
